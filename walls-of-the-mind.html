Click, Ka-Chunk! The sound of the door. I jumped, startled. Then swiftly tense. An unsettling presence Makes me shudder all over. Laboured breathing, in, out. Lonely Introvert. Tense. Abnormal. The walls seem to speak. Heavy Thudding. Flight or Fight? I choose flight. Standing still. Nothing moves. Or so it seems. Terrified. Fear washing over my body. But why? Click, Ka-Chunk! The sound of the - … Thud. Thud. Thud, I’m in free-fall Splotches of red Alongside me. Now, Nothing moves.
Pushed. Into a room we called home. Trapped In a home that is just a house. You Who pushed me in there Smiling in my pain And dancing on my grave. Clink. Sound of the lock. Wails. Sound of my voice. Walls. Speaking to me. Ba-bump. Sound of my heart. Wiped away Like a smear on your clothing. No windows. You won’t let me see daylight. No air You won’t let me breathe. No people Letting me rot here. Locked door. As if I wasn’t separate enough. Eyes heavy. Watching my life scurry away. Long lost hope. Like the ray of sunlight That doesn’t hit me. Love Like the way you THREW me Into this prison. Key. Like how you burned it And will burn me Into a living hell.
Hyperfixation. Rewind. Again in my head, again. Memories, flashbacks. Flowing, never stopping. Ideas roll. More and More. Failing, Questions. Answers. Formal. Casual. Brake pedal. Gas pedal. Pressed at the same time. Stop. Go. Breathe. Choke. Night Owl. Morning Lark. Eyes forgetting what sleep once was. No-one beside me. Except the Walls that speak With no-one to listen. Breathe. Hold. Try not to choke. (Un)happy cry. That no-one hears. You’re BACK? Trapped. Screaming silence. Scream. Pound. Escape reality. Ba-bump. Pounding again On the walls that chain me And you who love Every moment of it.
Icy COLD metal bites my hands. Not cold- Too cold it feels WARM. One drop of water falls at a time. Not from the sky But from my EYES. Warming the chains significantly. Pound. On thick grey walls That envelop my conscience, An injured conscience, But still a conscience. At least I have one Compared to those Who chain me. Escape? A window, with no bars. Glass that can be broken. 1… 2… 3…! I smash into it. Cuffs gone, hands bloodied and bruised. The glass shattered, shards spilling everywhere And I’m free. Run. Into a grey forgotten maze. Hope they chase me. And turn into something Only the maze can remember. Because they deserve it- I hear screaming from the other side. And a growing sensation wells up inside of me That only one person Is leaving alive.
Burning. Through a home I once knew. Ashes and ashes piling to the stars. Orange flames flickering, Their glow illuminating the layers of darkness. And me? Stuck inside of a room I once called my own. I wake. Hands cuffed But the flames eat through the metal. Yank. Scream. Pull. My hands burn off. With the cuffs that chained me. Agony. Seething hatred. But for now, I have to wait. I kick the door open. Unable to open my eyes. But in the smoke? I would go blind. I blindly stumble forward. Wherever coldest. Woah! I hit the ground And calm down. As I inhale the scent of flower. I watch. As my home Burns down And a part of me Burns down as well. The empty space within me Will hate whoever did this Forevermore.
Get a grip. I reach for the next handhold. Arms heavy, legs like jelly. Stretch. Body limp as a noodle. Keep pushing, keep pushing. Keep going. I look up. Sky blue sky Grey inky clouds. So far to go! I need motivation. Something that can keep me going. So I Look down. But… I see everything. Too far down. Feeling heavy. Stomach churning Head lolling. Breathe, gasping for air. Dizziness takes over My floating body. Get a grip! I float Falling Seemingly in slow motion. Watching everything Rise above me. The ground comes up to meet me But when I expect pain It's painless… Or too much for me To handle.
Crystal clear water. Arms swinging. Legs tensing. Ready to jump. Laboured breathing. Just a dive. Just a dive. To touch the bottom of the deep pool. Jump. I’m in freefall. Legs tight Arms launching. THUD. I slam into the water. My momentum carried me to the bottom. Now I have to make it back… Chest heaving. Lungs burning. Kick. Glide. Kick. I look up. So far away. I take a breath… And all I inhale is water. I slow down. As if going through mud. Heavy under the weight of the water. Arms dead. Eyes blurry. I kick upward with the last of my air Praying that it isn’t My Last Breath. Gasp.
Walls envelop my home Walls envelop my house. Grey tall walls That reach to the sky. Trapping. Closing. I try to scream But they block my sound. Walls fall down Burning in flame. The floor opens up And turns to water. I sink down Slowly. Slowly. Like sinking in mud. The water disappears But the blue stays. Skies of blue- My mind clouds over And I focus inward. Go. Stop. Go. Stop. Breathe. Chok- Breathe. I fall Down And Down. But I. Can Control My mind. No walls. No falling. I choose Doors that I can open.
