Hi! Smiling, standing At the door. Cheeky grin Plastered smile. Please And thank you. They're sitting down. 'Look at me!' 'I'm so great' 'Why didn't you do that?' 'Imagine being you?' Their voice echoes When I feel I'm not Enough. I Don't dislike them. I Loathe them. Still For now, I'll keep Smiling. Laugh the pain Off. 'Yeah we're friends!' I said to them, Don't worry! I'll place a bed of Roses at your funeral. Although I might have caused it.
Angel of white Lips of red. Starstriking laugh Piercing gaze. Perfect. Open ended Torture I try, I try I try like some comforting lies I see your face everywhere. Mall, sipping a mundane coffee. Road, parallel parking. School, talking with Friends. Awe struck- You ever care To look at me. Eyes white as daisies. Perfect teeth All in a row. Angel of white I just hate you lately I feel your laugh Crawling down my spine. Watching me Being PERFECTLY Still Watching them Hail.
I try to speak. Words jumble Catching in my throat. Each time I try To say hi Smile It forces its way Back down my throat. I wish I had A charming smile. A voice people can listen Forever to. I wish I could Speak to others. I will try. I work up the courage And willpower To say hi To me In the mirror. Bathroom door closed Mirror clear… … The words stay stuck in my throat. They always Have. Even to me I remain Silent.
I wish I had friends. Friends to laugh with Friends to cry with. I wish I had friends. People who supported me. I want friends like yours. People who can joke Around and be happy. I want friends like yours. You who has A shoulder to cry on. I think I have friends People who don’t Or can’t notice me. I think I have friends Who moved away Leaving me behind. And now all I have are crickets Barely touching the silence Barely heard Barely seen And soon I’ll be just like them. Just. Like. Them.
‘And this person who will win’ ‘Has all the moves.’ ‘And the winner for the citizenship award Goes to…’ Everyone’s heartbeat Can be felt across the room. Everyone tense Bodies stiff Eyes darting Who could it be? Then The announcer Dramatically points To… Me. I look shocked. Everyone else does too. I stare at the ground Then Slowly Stare up And smile. Still No one else is smiling. No one else Cares. I got what I wanted. Yet not what I imagined.
Months fly by Like moths to a flame. Rumors sprouted And died. My words Silent as ever. ‘HELLLOOOOO!!!’ Not. Again. People like this Barely any room To speak To breathe. Even other people Can’t get a word in. I do wonder If they feel like me- ‘If you had to permanently replace your hands with two common kitchen tools, which tools would you pick and how would you live your life? A friendly ghost agrees to do one specific, mundane household task for you forever. What do you ask for? If clouds came in distinct food flavors, what flavor should today's sky be?’ … Even my thoughts Aren’t this fast. ‘DID YOU HEAR THE LAST ONE???’ ‘I THINK TODAY IS..’ ‘BLAH BLAH BLAH’ Still I’m very much Here.
“So today class We will be doing a game A Blooket! So please get out your~” My thoughts finish the sentence. Finally! Some games after today. Some light at the end of the Darkness. I reach into my bag- ‘MISSSSSSS’ ‘WE FORGOT THE HOMEWORK’ Oh. Ugh. I forgot they were in The clas- “Oh thanks!” The entire class Groans. Oh. Ugh. Somehow I agree. Who is this kid??? Even though I can’t get a word in No words are Necessary. Pure Undiluted Loathing.

I’m holding on a rope Got me 10 feet Above the ground. I hear what people say But I can’t make a sound. The rope anchors me To people I know To people I knew. Past And future. Too bad there’s knots. Knots in my stomach Knots in the rope. Cuts where the ties should be. Knots pull at my stomach. Weighing me down Holding me Too close to the past. Ropes and Knots. Tying me down Lifting me up. Ropes. Holding too tight. Knots. Pulling me Back to the past Where I could Say hi.
Everyone sits on the mat. Ready for what comes next. Oh? We have to buddy up. For… Oh yay! A science fair- I look around. Be my buddy? Echoes around the room. I desperately try to ask. But the words stay silent. Pretty soon I’m left buddyless. And no one notices. Not the teachers. Not my ‘Friends.’ Not one Soul. Weeks pass. Rockets launching Music playing Plants grown. ‘And the winner is…’ Me?? Oh. I don’t care at this point. No one has.
Green grass moves Under our feet. Laughter fills the air Taking the place of Silence. Laying down. Enjoying the company When I’m not alone. My heavensent friend. Even when I quiet I swore I care. Friend. Friend… Years have slipped by Like the turning of a leaf. They're gone now. They found someone more exciting The next second They were gone Too fast Too soon. Leaving me quietly wondering If who they met was friendlier. Friendlier Than me. Friendlier Then what I could even be. Friendlier, What you were never.
‘Hi!’ The word comes out Releasing the griphold On my throat. It feels like The first word In forever. I finally Skip to school Amazed Delighted! And able To talk. I walk up To my classmate. … ‘Hi!’ … I wait for them to Respond. They give me A dirty look. And jog off. Agonizing Again Again. Every word Each friend.
