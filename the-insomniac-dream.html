This is the introduction for The Insomniac Dream. It is a chapbook based off of sleep, memory, dreams, and so much more. it consists of 5 poems, and if you countr the intro poem, that totals up to six. These poems were chosen not because of the dreams, but because of where the mind can take you when it's not fully awake. It was very fun to write and imagine all sorts of weird thngs that could happen. I hope you enjoy reading this!
Soft night sky. Mellow lampshade. Eyes slowly closing. Sleep whispers in my mind. Sleep. Sleep. My eyes close My thoughts disappear. Zzzzzzz… I dream, full of colour. Vivid red, living green. Paint on a canvas. Swirling, shimmering. Starry night Brightest day. Zzzzzzz… Skies change. Calming sky blue Mellow sunset. Layers of darkness. Back to back. Warming sunrise Mellow midday. Zzzzzzz… Yawn! I stretch out of bed. Sunlight streams through the room. I want breakfast.
I creep into the kitchen. Guilty searching for a snack. Eyes searching up and down the pantry. Struggling to see in the dimly lit room. Aromas entering and exiting my brain. Something sweet, something savoury? My hands search, and find something yum. A box of cookies open to eat. I eat, heartily, enjoying the sweet relief and taste. One at a time, enjoying each bite. So scrumptious I feel like falling asleep here… THUD … My head bangs on the floor. Eyes rolling around my head. Too dizzy to move, stand, or speak Into the silence around me. Brain too fuzzy. I CAN’T sleep here … Then, in the height of it all Something seems to move. Shadows collide. The dimly lit room seems to speak. Something comes closer. Can’t move, can't breathe. Choking on the food I once wanted. It comes even closer still … The light itself flickers, then shuts off. Leaving me and It in total darkness. And I swear I heard a second breath.
Warmth. I lie there, near the bedside table. Blind in the darkness. Slowly drifting farther. From the grasp of reality. Gentle river. Dissipating storm. Breathe. In, out, in out. Falling. Grasping onto thin air. Scream. Stomach churning, Ground coming up to meet me. Just before - THUD! I hit the ground. And my mind takes me somewhere else. I land in a room. Grey, bare walls envelop me. They tighten, me in the middle. I pound on the walls, THUD THUD THUD Shrinking faster. No end, no escape. Grey walls tinged with red blood. Wheeze! I bolt upright. Sure that I was doomed. Eh? I’m still here. I look at my hands. Not bruised or bloodied. But now.. I can’t go back to sleep. So I huddle in the tight bed. And my mind drifts off.
Yawn! I stretch out of bed. And gaze out my window. Pale orange sky Dancing white clouds. Sigh. Innocent, pure morning. Still. Thoughts And ghosts Wind up my brain. Starting files. Logging on. Rebooting memories… The waking nightmare. Thoughts cloud my brain. Like the sky outside. Swirls of thoughts Traveling down to my gut. Bees sting my stomach. Forcing poison into my veins. I can’t wake up. I don’t want to. It is much better here And safer! So I lay my head On a bed of clouds. Good Night! Or Morning.
Honk! Headlights shine Yellow and white. Winding road. Dark blue car, Small rear view mirror. Wait… It’s darker in the mirror Than outside… Home. White tall walls spiraling to the stars. Yellow streetlight bright as the stars. Home Sweet Home. Except… All the houses Are dark. And yet… Something is watching. Me. Click. Key turning. Wrong way. Click. Shivers run down my spine. But then… Clink! I’m inside. But… I left the lights on So why are they off? Tap. Tap. Tap. Coming from My bedroom. Not going in. Run. A room with a lock. PLEASE! Bathroom. Ka chink! Locked. Tap. Tap. Tap. I stare at the mirror But when I look I’m smiling. Tap. Thump. Taps mixed in with my heartbeat. Tap. TAP. TAP. And the mirror breaks. And something comes out. It smiles at Me.
