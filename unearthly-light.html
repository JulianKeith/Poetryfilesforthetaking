Cloudless blue sky. The world bathes in warmth. New life awakens. Gaze up to a smiling sun. Below the meadow, bustling city. All ready, just for today. Crimson and saffron fill the sky. Blinding all in its gaze. Street lights flicker to life. Even their iridescent glow Screams orange and red. The sun moves below the horizon. But a new light arises. The world bathes in silver. Sky, shades of blue and black. New life creeps back out. Unseen by all. Many thousands of constellations, And a heavensent landscape Shaped to perfection. Start again. Sunlight streaking my face. Moonlight bathing my back. A half, and half. Perfect harmony. One light dims, One light newly blinds all. Yawn.
Skies can darken. Eyes of white Blanket of black. Terrify the mind and Soul. Monstrosities can roam In these muddled times. Skies can brighten. Clouds of white Sheets of blue. Keeping the people Awake Awake. Skies can rain. Pouring sheets of Water. Ice. Light. Destroying millions Of millions Of millions. Skies can become Orange. Blue skies To orange skies. Creaking swing. Childhood dream. Orange glow. Past of the past Love of the flow.
Dusk till dawn. Amber light piercing Through layers of darkness. Illuminating the street. Grey pavement basking in light. A brown bench And me, waiting for dawn. Breathe. Time moves. Fast as the birds Slow as the feathers. Moon racing across the sky. Twilight becomes midnight The amber light Like a mini dawn. But I don’t notice The subtle flickering That the light emits. Flick. Flic- I am left stranded In layers of darkness. Pitch black. Breathe- That isn’t my breath. I sprint. Down a street I once knew. Huff. Puf- I fall. Knees bloodied and battered. I look up weakly. Await my fate When an orange glow Brightens the street. Illuminating The city.
The sky turns black. A blanket covers all. Sheets go over me. Over me from head to toe. They cover me, cover me, cover me. It seems so endless. Ghosts scream, breathe, howl. I can almost hear them next to me. I try to scream back. Something shoves me. Softly at first. Then harder. I fall over. Trying to get back up. The lights start to flicker. Over here, over there, over there. Something roars in response. The lights flicker again. Slowly at first. Then faster. Until all the lights flicker out. The sheets pour down more slowly. Slower, slower, slower. Ghosts seem to move the blanket. Holes appear, slowly at first, then more, more, more. The holes in the blanket grow bigger. So big that there is no more blanket. Blue flows in.
Step out, twilight journey. Walk, step after step. Sun goes down. Moon comes - It is dark out, with no moon. The sky is layers and shades of grey. And many shades of black. Walk alone Stumbling on the path before me. Stumbling in the silence That envelops all words. Tripping blindly. Struggling to scream for help. Inside the layered nightmare. I stumble, then trip. Falling down, weightless. Splotches of red emerge, And my vision tainted red. Still blinded, still helpless, still hopeless. And I gaze up in despair. At the darkness above me. Staring up. Asking why I am alone. Waiting for a sign, anything. Then, something appears. Tiny dots, glimmer, sliver and white. Contrasted to the black before it. Like a woven tapestry. Even in the absence of the moon, The stars come out to shine.
Ominous. Light shut off from the world. Bathing in darkness. Ethereal beauty as a halo appears. But this time something feels cold. Something feels off. Darkness shrouds the world And it feels everlasting. The moon devours the sun. No halo, and it seems no sun. Layers of darkness, and a grey outline That is our moon. Behind me Grey light shuts off the world. Not bright silver, dull shades of grey. The moon dances in the shadows. Eyes adjusting, and readjusting. Grey? Black? The umbra is too dark to see. Is this forever? It is too cold to think … Frigid, ice grows on every surface. Unearthly, the light sucked out of this world. Death. The sun hiding behind a rock That cannot be the moon. At all costs. Nothing moves. Except for the shivers That run down everyone's spine.
Layers of darkness over a desolate city. Moonlight streaking across the plains. A sliver of light shining over all. Stars swept away by the smoke. And all electricity disappears Lights flickering as the silver sets in. The darkness envelops all And what it does not overtake Is shone on by the silver light. No clouds to block it. No stars to drown it out. It stagnates over everything. The bustle of the city has disappeared. All life stuck inside of the plain grey walls. Everything is washed in grey. Under the unnerving silver light. The darkness seems to fade As the light illuminates more and more. And more. … The moon comes into sight. As the water from the pipes bursts out. Only to be dragged upwards But fall back. The buildings are floating. And gravity snaps. The moon is rapidly growing. And I see in it all its ruptured beauty Before it smashes into the city. The shattered moon And a shattered heart. The broken moon Over my broken window. The beautiful moon And beautiful sadness. Grey moon, grey sky. My world where everything is left grey.
Luminous. Rays of glimmering light fill the sky. The world bathes in its warmth. Its light gleams onto every surface. The light dazzles everyone who looks at it. It shines for all. Over a clear blue sky. Silver. Something small races across the sky. Barely visible. Sliver in the blue. It roams around In the sky again. But oddly, in the daytime. For what purpose, who knows. Silence. The birds stop singing. The bees stop humming. The world grows dark. The moon has covered the sun. In a Halo Of Darkness. And nothing moves. The moon seems to shrink. And the halo intensifies. Water crashes into the buildings. Flowing over the meadows. The moon is moving away. The tides sink into the city. The sun and moon grows And the light and shadows collide To break apart at a colossal scale. Light returns to the world. From ink black To a dull shade of grey.
