What are my Minor Chapbooks?

My minor chapbooks are chapbooks that fall under the realm of My Ghosts Of Tommorow, which was the opening poem. Each chapbook is a branch of My Ghosts of Tommorow, and reflect each paragraph that was in the poem. These poems are the headers foer the chapbook, and I hope you have a great time reading not just these, but the poems that follow these. Please do check out poems, as the links for them are over there.
Exhale. My breath fogs up, White as the mountaintops. Cold as The Winter Sun. Grass blades tinged with ice, And a dome of cold surrounding me. Freezing, Biting, Cold. The ice melts to rain, That pours down in sheets. Grass grows, leaves bloom, Flowers blossom, buds open. Animals wake, new family beside them. All is calm, all is still. Like the heartbeat of nature itself. Grey clouds open, blue flows in. The summer sun, so warm and bright. Heat pours out, warm delight. Lounging outside, sand beneath my toes. Olive green leaves, bearing fruit. Seeing smiles, brighter than daylight Warmer than warm. Green leaves wither to yellow. Trees turn bare, Brown oak against the pile of dead leaves. Animals back to hiding, School over again. Watching time fly, just as the birds Who don’t fly no more. As if their wings were cut.
Eyes closing amid the darkest nights, An endless cascade of noise. All inside, all inside, always inside. A quiet face, but a loud inner space. Tossing and turning, disturbing the outer silence. Close your eyes, Close your eyes. Lungs rise, lungs fall, Escaping silence’s deadly grasp. Listening, inhale, exhale. Struggling to relax, tense as an unsettling presence Appears, drawing breath away. Close your eyes, Close your eyes. A second breath befouls the air. Its cold breath radiates fear. Eyes flutter open, at the slightest sound Frozen in fear - No. This is worse. A shadow, and you. Fear overwhelms, taking the place of silence. Close your eyes, Close. Your. Eyes. Eyes tight shut (Coming closer.) Breathing (Inhale) Trying to sleep (Even closer) Still alive (Not for long) Who’s this? (It’s you) Screaming. Cursing. Crying. Until I can move no longer.
Run, legs shaking. Arms trembling. Breathe. Through the pain, into the pain. Intersection. Turn right, Bear left. Keep moving. Wrong direction, dead end. Turn around, facing it. Screaming. Cursing. Sprinting. It smiles. Gruesome. Horrid. Sprint the other way. Hoping it can’t see me. Pray the dim lights save me. Silence. It is around the corner. Control my breathing. Not speaking. Thinking as hard as I can. It confronts me. Did… It just read my thoughts? Huff. Puff. EXIT AHEAD. Run! FASTER!!!! I step through the door. And it can’t follow. Safe. But… I see. Another intersection.
Shimmer. Reality blurs. Light fractures. Warping, rupturing, moving. Silver in the black, Woven into reality. Light, yet no sun or moon. Dim street, dark city. Stars. Glimmering, twinkling. Dots on a black page. Little lampshades. White as a winter breath. Cold as a Winter Sun. Nature at its finest. No walls to block me here. One star is brighter than the rest. One star that illuminates the world. One star that grows bigger. That… Comes closer. One star. That takes the place of the sun. This is not the sun anymore. This is different. Luminous light. Pitch black dark. White specks over a black page. Layers of darkness. One dot of light, a spectrum of grey. A rock we call the Moon And a star that isn’t the sun. Unearthly light flourishes.
Shift. Day in, day out. Walking into the class. Feeling eyes up And down My spine. Breathe. New year, new me, new class. Sigh, inhale. So tired. 10 weeks, 30 to go. Day in, day out. Nine till three. Breathe. No one looks anymore. Just another face. Hi! Brightening up. 20 weeks, 20 to go. Day in, day out. People speak, People greet. I am another friend To wave and say hi to! Wassup! Peak of the year. 39 weeks, one more day. People laugh, people cry. Moving to college. Staying behind. Watch them leave And wishing I leave with them. My fault for being a year too young.
One. One move. One. One heartbeat a second. Ba Bump, Ba bump. One plus one Is definitely two. Exam. Test questions. Ba bump. Easy. Logical. Ace! Perfect. Just again. And again. And again. But then One wrong word Makes up for 1000 good words. One sad face Makes me long for so many ones. One wrong choice And I spiral backwards 100 fold. Does One + One Still equal Two? Boom. Boom Boom. Not one thought a minute But many thoughts a minute. Not one heartbeat a second Ba bump. Ba-bump. Bump. And maybe, just maybe One plus one Could be three.
Pen in hand. What to do? Staring at the page. Blank white Empty canvas. Inspiration… ARISE! Still nothing. Thoughts hum In the back of my mind. Origami? Another poem? No… I want… Something Sweeter… What to make? I look around. Candy? Chocolate? No… ‘Hi?’ I whip around. Reality blurs. I stare into Caramel brown eyes. I now know What to write. I now know Who I want to spend time with. Always Baking trays Of Dear Desserts.
