What is this?

This is a website dedicated to showing my poems and the inspirations behind them!

What are poems?

Poems are a form of literature that uses line breaks and rhythmic patterns to convey emotions and ideas.

Here is one example I wrote myself:

Breathe. A presence enters the room. I inhale. But… I seem to inhale something more than air. Something new comes from within. A presence that now comes from within. Pulling strings. Watching me. And it takes me somewhere new… Ba-bump. Heart beating, lungs shaking Arms trembling, legs burning. Exhale. Tension within And outside of my body. In this pressured mind. And out. But then walls surround my body And mind. Run. Sprint down a corridor. Turn right, leading to an exit. Into a burning house. Escape the flames To see another intersection. Another part of the maze. With walls that speak to all. But I remember I’m not alone. The walls fall down Which reveals lush green trees. Flowers that bloom brightly Next to a winding river And under a ray of sunlight. Brown oak, green leaves. Spring leaf to summer flower. Breathe. Print(“The trees fade”) Print(“And turn into ones and zeros.”) Print(“The flowers turn pixelated”) Print(“And the forest goes silent”) Print(“Other than the hum of machinery.”) Print(“But…”) Print(“I look over the forest”) Print(“And into a classroom.”) Riiing! Walking from my code to the door. Coming into a class. Writing lines, making friends. Ghosts of past times. Seeing faces, old and new. Making way for a new year. Hi! Hello! Walking, Running, Sprinting. My feet start to leave the ground. Rising up… Higher and higher. Through the roof, Above the sky. Whoosh. I see light and shadows colliding Like paint on a canvas. Stars like dots on a black page. But shivers Start to crawl down my spine… Take me down please… I shudder and shiver. No presence. No moon, no sun. No tree, no flowers. No people, and no ghosts. And yet… I see them in my mind And they flow into my brain And most importantly My words.
