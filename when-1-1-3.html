Test tubes Lay broken all around. But This is it. All that work. One last drop The last one. Sweat pouring from my skin Tears of joy. Drip. Drip. Two drops instead of one. It rumbles Then it stops. The violin whistles. Piano in the background. Working in harmony. Strumming a soft melody The guitar sounds sad But not lonely. Each instrument Played to the best. Tick. Tick. In beat. Tick. I step in. Voice adding to the sound. Sweet lullaby Soft trill. Words floating Soft airy notes That barely leaves my throat. The violin trembles. Piano keys slamming hard. The climax has arrived. A5… C#6… E6… A6… The last of the air Leaving my lungs. C#7 E7 A7.. But the sound doesn’t leave My throat.
