  • Pretty Happy for Depression
Stop. Go. Stop. Go. 3… 2… 1… Race… GO! Acceleration. Brake. Tyres skid to a halt. Stuck. Wait. Impatient. HONK. And I get to go again. YES! Drift. Skidding in the ground. Last turn. Slam hard on that pedal Swirl. Spiral. But then I press the brake And accelerator pedal At the same time. Which stunts the car And hurls me away. Causing me To spiral into the dust.
