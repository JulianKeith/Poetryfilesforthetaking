This is the intro for Nature's Seasons. It is a chapbook based off of nature. It ranges from flowers, to trees, to creation itself. This chapbook has 12 poems, and if we include Nature's Seasons, that becomes 13. The chapbook consists of four different parts, winter, spring, summer, and autumn. This was a very fun chapbook to write, and I hope you enjoy it too.
