This is the intro for Nature's Seasons. It is a chapbook based off of nature. It ranges from flowers, to trees, to creation itself. This chapbook has 14 poems, and if we include Nature's Seasons, that becomes 15. The chapbook consists of four different parts, winter, spring, summer, and autumn. This was a very fun chapbook to write, and I hope you enjoy it too.
Bathe. Outside In the harsh winter air. Feeling the sheets Pour down on my skin And the crystals Melt to the touch. Exhale. The breath fogging up The already pure air. I keep moving. The crystals keep falling. Gems twinkling And shimmering in the light. I gaze upward. Awestruck by The rarity And wonder of this. My body in motion My heart racing Stuck, yet going. But still The snow ceases to fall The cold settles to warm. Wind heaves against my back. Breathe But my breath is crystal clear. But on my shirt Is the last remains of The Snow Days.
Cold. Frigid snow that comes down in sheets. Ice that bites at my skin. Sun that gives no warmth. The peak as bright as day And the bottom covered by storms. One step, then another, then another. Sigh. Breath as white as the mountaintop. Breath as cold as the sun. The feeling of warmth Left behind at the bottom. The peak coming into sight. One step, then another, then another. The top of Earth. Standing above all. Above the snow, above the ice. Finally. … All over. Done. Inhale- My breath comes short … Have to get back down. FAST. Race to the bottom. Against the ice and snow. Pushing to the limit. Sprinting down. Beneath the clouds- … I’m in freefall. The mountain right behind me. And I land Aware of where I will go now.
Fragile. Pale yellow rays barely visible Between frigid white clouds That dance in the breeze. The brittle air cold to the bone Not listening to the yellow sun That gives no warmth. Deciduous trees, a life with no leaves. Red Autumn leaves raked away. White falling in sheets. Snow covering the oaken branches. Pure white over the dark oak brown. Gnarled shoots longing for leaves. A flutter of wings piercing the air. Little breaths stirring the sky. Bright crimson belly. Sky blue wings. Changing the air. The sun seems to listen. And warmth gradually sets in. Still fragile. Pale yellow rays barely visible Between frigid white clouds But the Winter sun Starts to give its warmth. The bird That dared to face the cold.
The flower blooms, the flower breathes. Basking in the sun. Yellow streaks through rosy buds. Watching it sprout, leaves shooting up. Light streaking through blue skies. Reflecting onto the flower below. Flower to sapling. Bursting with leaves. One sprout in millions. Still shining, still warm. Branches barely reaching my knee. Just waiting for it to grow. With it the whole way. The sun turns to rain. Blue over lush green. Water brushing against the leaves. It caresses the leaves, The leaves absorbing the droplets. Time can wait, I can wait. Waiting and caring With tender and gentle hands. The tree is here. Branches towering over the yard. Able to hug it, gone so far. Able to bask in its shade, Watching the leaves slowly fall. Life to Life, Leaf to leaf. And wait for the next flower Beneath The Sunlight’s Ray.
As I lay down in the meadow Caught guilty nodding to sleep A whiff of pollen floats to me The sweet scent inhaled, relief! If I could stay here Beneath the great old tree Yes. that would be a dream. My eyes flutter open. A sea of white, red and purple Opened up around me. How long was I asleep for? It has been too long, But for me, not long enough. A sweet scent, just for me? The problem is, it isn’t real. There is something more. Return me please, For pain makes us human And those without pain Are living an illusion. Back to the bustle of the city. Roar of the cars. Smoke drifting, yet no fire. Light, yet no sun or moon. And yet in the midst of it all, I catch a whiff of a flower.
Walking by the soft riverbank. In the midst of my childhood. Seeming to drift with the current. Watching it gently flow by, Babbling slower, and slower. Turning crystal clear. And seeing the fish in pristine clear water. Keep flowing. Fishes of all different sizes, shapes, and colours. Watching them move with the river. Watching the kelp softly sway, Dark green against the pure clear blue. Floating by, gliding by, flowing by. Night comes, night goes. The darkness unable to Separate me and the river. The river keeps flowing. Who knows where it will go? I wonder. I seem to come closer to Where it will go. I step once more. And can step no longer. The river flowed into a wide ocean. My feet soaking in the tide. Seaweed grows like grass. Fish playing in it. I look at my hands. And find those of an adult. I have flowed as well.
Rushing water. Clear as the morning sky. Flowing softly. Inhale. Drifting with the current. Gently flow. Watching the stream Carry worries away. The water stills, And my reflection appears. Not sorrowful, not elated. Content, peaceful, at ease. Still breathing. Still smiling. Genuinely. I look up, Watching the storm fade away. The beginning of a new day. The grey fades out, The blue settles in. No sign of the storm. No sign of the rain. Just white, pure clouds. Focus on breathing. Sigh, in and out. Not like a storm, But like clouds drifting away. They may drift apart, But that opens up The blue, calming sky. Breathing in me.
Flowers bloom, colours bursting to life. Radiant shades, regal colours, vibrant hues. Bees buzz, killing the silence with a soft melody. Their sweet scent carried across the meadow. Ants work diligently underground Their tunnels coming to the surface. When out of the blue… Something violently swarms my body. Grasping and stinging. Terror crawls out of the tunnel. A tidal wave of pure hatred. Biting. Stinging. Dying. Until I come crashing down. And everything fades. My eyes stay shut. But I get them open. I wished I hadn't. Flowers slowly wilting, no one cares for them. The ground hardens and cracks, no one uses it. The rain stops falling, green grass turns barren. My hard work all ruined. My life stagnating. Who am I now? I’m faded. Broken. Lost. Gone. Nothing moves. Still One moment Of clarity Helps And then. It happens. My memories start slowly falling over me Like a soft warm blanket. My cozy peace. My tranquility. My name. Who I am. What I like. Not overbearing, not overwhelming. Just coming, slowly at first, And not stopping until I remember everything. The good, The bad. And everything in between. And I'm content. Or am I?
Where the wind meets waves There’s sand. Heaps of Grains. Piling to the stars. Basking in the sun. Blow in the wind wet in the wave. Where the wind meets waves There’s Sun. Warmth gazing down. Melting my ice cream. Keeping me hot. Watching over all. Where the wind meets waves theres life. Moving, enjoying the Moment. Waves tapping wind blowing. Relaxing, an overly hot summer day. Where the wind meets waves There’s summer. Coconut trees Swaying in the wind Bearing good fruit. The feel of summer Where the wind Meets waves.
Sweat. Water that runs down my spine. Salt that drizzles to the floor. Just like the pouring rain. That comes down in sheets. Humid air That seems to breathe Alongside the mellow monsoon. Breathe. Air choked with water Gushing down like a river. The water coming to my ankles And wetting my already soggy legs. Trees that bend down To hit me. And wind that batters my face Like a gust of oven wind. The horizon A vast expanse of water The endless Pacific Ocean. But Something is coming. Fast. A spiral envelops the coast And throws everything into Chaos. Flying. I seem to glide along the wind Light as a leaf Although we have plenty of that too. A tree hits me in the face. And all goes black. THUD. I wake. Floating over water And the dead bodies. That cover everything.
Swirl. Fog creeps into the forest. Hiding trees that never left. Grey clings to all. Reality blurs. Clouds blanketing the sky And Mist blanketing the earth. Clouding my vision. Dense clouds of Mist Hovering over the surface. Almost dreamlike. Keep moving. I step forward, uneasy. My foot trips over a small stone. And the ground comes up to meet me. I get up. Knees bloodied and battered. I hit my head on a low branch. And get tangled in the branches. I’m stuck. Dangling from the tree Shrouded in mist. I called for help. I scream. No use. I pull a torch from my bag. And I flash it. The light reflecting onto the mist. And I am visible to all. Thanks to the mist. Dancing away.
Whoosh. Breeze whipping my hair. Ruffling the plants. Air shifting Moving. Clouds pass overhead. Dancing without a care. Whooosh. Wind sways the trees. Makes them wave hello. Puppets on a string. Entire fields sway left. Sway right. Almost floating away. Whoosh! Rain falls down in sheets. From every direction. Wind scatters everything Like playing with sand Or blowing on rice. Blow, blow, blow. … … Tossed around Like clothes in the wash. Camera ripped from my hands. Breath taken from my lungs. Floating, spinning. Watching the last of the ground Falling away.
Everything God makes Always has a purpose Crimson and saffron fill the sky. Light comes from the west. Blinding all in its heavenly glow. The light finally dims. But a new light arises. Making the darkness brighter. Everything God makes Always has a Purpose. The world turns dark. Shades of everything blue. The lights fade from sight Look up. A shooting star traces across the sky. Look down. The same star is reflected in a watery glow. Just like a halo. Everything God makes Always has a Purpose. Buds unfurl slowly. Revealing colours inside. Every shade of Red and Pink Every shade of Orange and Yellow Just like miniature suns. A flame of hope in the darkness. Everything God makes Always has a purpose. Something bursts from the ground Everything burns around it. The moon fades out. The trees fall down. The earth cracks open. The birds fall, lifeless. The flowers wilt and turn grey. Everything dies. Does everything God make Always have a purpose?
