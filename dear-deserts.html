Ding! The sound of the oven Chimes. Piping hot goods Steam out in the dozens. Aromas waft through the room. I breathe in the scent. She hands one out to me. ‘Care to try one?’ I take a quick peek. Pink glossy icing Black chocolate whiskers. ‘It’s cute!’ She pouts, cheeks turning red. ‘I’m not that cute! I take a bite. Mmm! It’s choc-filled with icing. Sugar on sugar. Pinky goodness! I take another bite. Still so good! I turn to tell her ‘This is great!’ But when I look up She’s gone! Leaving me To munch on my Cupcake.
Swelter under the steaming sun. Sand scorching my toes. The grains getting in between my toes. The waves lap against the shore. Bright Endless blue sky. Sweat running down my spine. She reaches a hand out. ‘Come with me?’ I grab on. Where could she take me? We sprint along the beach. Hand in hand Running in harmony. ‘Hi!’ The cart owner says. She asks for 2 ice creams. One vanilla ice cream And … She stares at me. Caramel brown eyes gazing at mine. Uhhh… ‘One caramel ice cream!’ The attendant smiles. The scent of caramel Washes through the air- Here you go! I take my ice cream. Mmmm! It tastes great! I look at her ice cream It also looks good! So we happily Eat our Ice creams.
She smiles. ‘Bake a cake with me?’ I joyfully… Accept. She pulls out A bare brown cake. Steaming in the sun. Cakey aroma floating in the air. She pulls out some icing. White. Pink. So many. So sweet!. Squirt! Icing pipes out- Splat! Some falls onto her hand. She laughs. I laugh- ‘HEYYYYY!’ She rubs the icing onto my face. It's smooth. And slightly cool. Hpmh! I grab the pipe And squirt it on her face. ‘AHHH!’ She laughs again. A cute trill Like a hummingbird. Oh yes. The cake. We smooth And roll And squirt And the cake Is finished.
