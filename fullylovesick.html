Autumn leaves fall Slowly to the ground. Slabs of grey concrete Make way to An enormous hall. I gaze up, Over the- A solid thump! I collide into Something solid. I fall. Straight onto my Back. “Yeouch!” I look around Curious Of who I bumped into. Oh~ It’s a Cute girl Um… Ehehe I freeze up. Um… My thoughts go Blank. I reach a hand. She grabs on.
School bell rings. Time for lunch. Line up- I'm right behind Her. Long wavy black hair In my face. I work up All of my willpower. 'Excuse me?' She whips around. Fast as the wind. "Yeah?" She seems to Stare into my Soul. "I'm s-sorry for e-earlier!" She pauses. Caramel brown eyes Gazing at Mine. She laughs. A short trill. 'Oh that?' 'It's fine!' My heart pounded Undoubtful Of something...
“Hey dude!” He jogs right to me. Across the street. This is my neighbour- “I SAID HELLO???” “ARE YOU IN THERE??” ‘YES I’M HERE.’ ‘SHUT U-’ I try to finish my sentence. He won’t let me. He never has. Still. He’s quite a fun- “YES SO YOU WERE LIKE” “SO DARN AWKWARD WITH HER.” “WHY?” “WAS IT THE HAIR” “THE BREATH” “THE SMILE??” “PLEASE RECONSIDER YOUR MARRIAGE-” … ‘MARRIAGE????’ Hmph. Well. ‘AT LEAST I HAVE OPTIONS.’ ‘YOU ARE HOPELESS- Oh well. “YEAH SHUT IT.” On the contrary I won’t.
