A fire in their eyes, And a fire in their heart. Still warm, still open. Who could this be? Come closer. Look inside. Are they crippled? Do they care? Come closer, look inside … Who could this be? Keep it all together. Even through 50 Shades Of Grey. Who’s this person? Where’s their warmth? Outer devotion? All I see is a vision, a wish. But still. I think I know Who this could be …
Pant! Adrenaline courses through my body. Tired from The Daily Run. Uphill, downhill, straight. Huff. Puff. Jogging my heart out And it seems my legs too. Whoosh. Someone new overtakes me. Ugh. 5th to 6th. Nope. Not on my watch. Energy fades as I sprint for a bit From 6th to 4th. YESSS! Feet barely touch the ground. The end is in sight. I can imagine myself crossing it. First, second, third I sprint. Praying I can overtake someone. ANYONE. One more step… Last one … YESS. Black shoes over red line. Blue clothes covering drops of sweat. Don’t care. One goal achieved, but how much more to go? Still I bask in the victory More than 2nd place could ever. Or fourth Because I barely made it.

Jul 16, 2026 A Friend Who Goes I remember … The sound of the school bell. Ringing, chiming, tolling. Peace of the mind. Running back to the classroom. With someone I care about. And who cares about me. I remember … The sound of your voice. Talking, chatting, laughing. Noise that speaks to my heart. Peace of the mind. Nothing can separate us right? … I remember … The day you left. Sad tears, happy cry. You said Forever and Always But … Did you mean it? Because now … A space in my heart where you used to sit. A silence your chatter filled up. A painful question that burns my brain. And an answer that I refuse to say. No more waiting at the door. No more saving spots on the mat. You’re moving miles away When now, I can’t take a single step.

Breathe. Crimson light spills onto the horizon. Illuminating the beach. Sand between my toes Waves lapping onto the shore. Away from the city bustle. When suddenly I feel something gently touch my hand. Or someone. I look. Her hand intertwines with mine. Her caramel brown eyes Staring at mine. Bump. Ba-Bump. I stare back. And she comes closer. Still giving me her unwavering gaze. Ba-bump. Her lips over mine. My head in the clouds. Ba-bump. Eyes closed Enjoying the moment- Of a first kiss. Under a sunset glow. She pulls back. Her warm smile still there Her caramel eyes locked into mine. Ba-bump. The waves still lapping onto the shore The sun still smiling down at us. And I gently Float back to Earth.
Walk. Step after step. Life walks. I walk. The footprints Left behind In the sand. We walk. As friends side by side. But then you fall Knees bloodied and battered. So I reach out A hand to help But I can now tell You have only looked At the grazes on your knee And because I now know I am just a pawn In your eyes The strength To take more steps Drains out of me. Silver turns to grey. Red to brown. Life keeps going Even though I stopped. A long time ago. And no one noticed. Or maybe cared. Traitor.
